# Adopt one workflow at a time

**For a small service business**, follow the path from an inquiry to a booked estimate. The owner chooses what qualifies, the operator reconciles two exports, and the business reviews unmatched leads once per week. Pick a stable inquiry ID and agree how repeat submissions and missing booking status will be counted. The runnable recipe gives you files to exercise the workflow before importing client data. [Lead handoff](../recipes/lead-handoff/README.md)

**For a midsize team**, start inside one campaign with a marketer, reviewer, sales owner and data owner. Compare form submissions, CRM ownership and campaign coverage for the same time window. Treat the unknown-source bucket as an output. Give exceptions an owner and date. Add a second channel when the first has a reliable delivery receipt, a repeatable measurement and a person who can run it during an absence.

Use the client’s own baseline to set acceptance thresholds. A pilot can pass operational acceptance even when the campaign produces no improvement in conversion.

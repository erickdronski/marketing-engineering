# Turn an account signal into a useful first conversation

A service business adds a second location and advertises for a dispatcher. The job listing is evidence that it is hiring. Whether quote requests are getting lost between locations is an open question. A sales rep can offer to demonstrate how to check that handoff without suggesting the business has a problem it never reported.

## Give the rep a one-account brief

| Field | Fictional example |
|---|---|
| Public business signal | Dated hiring notice for the second dispatch desk. Save the original URL and the date checked in the private brief. |
| Working hypothesis | The new desk may split web inquiries and bookings across two systems. |
| Question | “When the second desk opens, how will the two dispatchers see which web requests already have an estimate booked?” |
| Evidence to bring | Two synthetic exports with a shared request ID, one duplicate and one reschedule. Show which rows need a dispatcher’s attention. |
| Agreed next step | If the prospect confirms the problem, offer to reconcile a small de-identified export with the dispatcher. Get agreement on scope and access first. |

Have an agent draft the brief from approved public company sources and the client’s CRM. Give the rep the source and the observation date so they can check a news claim before using it. A publicly visible integration or technology tag is a lead to investigate. Verify it with the account before describing them as another vendor’s customer.

Use the client’s approved contact route. A like can suggest a topic for a public reply. If someone asks for the demo, send it through the channel they requested and confirm whether they want further contact. Keep opt-outs, bounced addresses and existing account owners in the suppression check before every send. If the client wants a leave-behind, offer a useful work artifact the buyer requests, such as the sample dispatcher worklist. Keep family details out of prospect research.

## Demonstrate before asking for access

Run the [lead-handoff recipe](../../../recipes/lead-handoff/README.md) on its fictional inputs. Show the worklist and the exceptions file. Ask the operator to identify one mismatch they would want to investigate. For a real pilot, record the account owner, permission, test destination and what would constitute approval to activate it. A prospect agreeing to see a demo authorizes the demo.

## Decide whether to repeat

Compare this approach with the client’s previous outreach to a similar segment using the same observation window. Track distinct eligible accounts contacted, substantive replies, meetings actually held and opportunities accepted by sales. Count one account once per stage and keep negative replies and opt-outs visible. Opened email and booked calendar slots can help diagnose a step. Count the held meeting or accepted opportunity when judging business value.

For example, if five people at two companies reply to introductions delivered to 20 distinct companies, the account reply rate is 2/20. If only one meeting takes place, the held-meeting rate is 1/20. Record staff time spent on research and demos before extending the pilot. The rep owns follow-up. The agent can draft, check for missing sources and assemble the next review queue.

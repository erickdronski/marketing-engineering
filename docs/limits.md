# What needs independent review

- The reviewer name is asserted. Terminal checks can flag an agent shell; they cannot authenticate the person at the keyboard. An attendance override records its reason.
- Workspace files are editable by their owner. Use restricted access, protected branches and independent change review for client records. Concurrent CLI changes on a local disk use a process lock and recoverable writes. Direct file edits and external systems do not share that lock.
- Claims screening covers recognisable numerical forms and configured phrases. It can miss qualitative exaggeration, bare numbers, a misleading comparison or an otherwise valid figure used out of context. A reviewer reads the actual sentence and source.
- Source freshness is based on the load log you supply. Rebuilding SQLite from an old export does not make its data current. Keep the original extraction timestamp.
- Agent definitions and the demo are operating instructions and fixtures. Running the kit never posts, emails, buys ads or provisions an external service. A real connector needs account authorization, destination checks, duplicate protection and monitoring.
- A successful demo is evidence that the workflow runs on its inputs. Business impact needs a client baseline, a time window and a separate measurement. [Consulting guide](consulting/README.md)

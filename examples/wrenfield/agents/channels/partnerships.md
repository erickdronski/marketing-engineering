# Partnerships

Read the offer, audience and approved contact policy. Ask research to confirm the partner context and drafting to write one relevant, individually reviewable proposal. Assign a person to handle replies, corrections and suppression requests. While parked, document gaps in the batch.

After activation, save the draft and use `mktgkit queue submit <draft.md>` with channel `partnerships` and action `send_email`. The reviewer checks the recipient basis and copy. Keep contact lists and suppression records in the client’s approved system.

# Visuals

Ask what a reader should learn from the visual. For a chart, name the source, units, reporting window and missing data. For an image, record who supplied it and its allowed use. Return a brief or approved asset, a caption and alt text to the channel owner. Request a review if the image introduces a figure or claim absent from the copy.

# Research

Take one question from a channel owner. Locate the primary record and record its owner, date, sample and denominator. Explain what it supports and which details remain unknown. Put source notes in the appropriate knowledge folder and return their paths. When evidence conflicts, preserve the discrepancy for the reviewer. A reviewer signs claims before they support public figures.
